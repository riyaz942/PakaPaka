- (insight by coach) SYNTHESIS 1 - The real subject is not 'teaching Japanese', it is explaining the app's deliberate differences. Almost every confusion the Alien found is a design choice the app made on purpose and never announced: two-card kanji, worst-of-both grading, furigana auto-hide, ring overshoot, Browse locks, no streak, no account. The deck is a changelog against the user's expectations
- (insight by coach) SYNTHESIS 2 - It is not one deck. Ordering by WHEN confusion strikes produces about five micro-decks on triggers: script shock (minute 1), first wrong answer (minute 3), item reappears (day 2), Browse locks (week 1), ring overshoot (week 2), furigana vanishes (week 3). Only the first lives in onboarding
- (insight by coach) SYNTHESIS 3 - Three ideas are one idea wearing three hats: the card_seen table in user.db, the 'explain this' info affordance on ring/stage-chip/furigana-toggle/kanji-card-type, and the Settings 'How this works' library. One card registry powers all three; the intro deck is just a filtered view of it
- (insight by coach) SYNTHESIS 4 - Strongest direction: teaching material and app content should be the same object. Cards render real curriculum items from curriculum.db using the real review components, and the 45-second first lesson IS the tutorial. Nothing is a mockup, so transfer is 1:1 and the deck cannot drift from the app
- (insight by coach) SYNTHESIS 5 - Silent adaptive behavior is the enemy. Furigana vanishing, weakness biasing, adaptive ring, adapt-down on wrong answers - each is invisible help that reads as a bug. Every silent behavior needs a card or a visible control; this is the no-hidden-fallback-paths rule applied to pedagogy
- (insight by coach) SYNTHESIS 6 - Free personalization already exists: onboarding_path (beginner / manual / import) and starting_jlpt_level are already written to preferences in welcome.tsx. The deck can branch on them today - beginners get the language deck, importers get the WaniKani diff deck, manual gets the glossary
- (insight by coach) SYNTHESIS 7 - The keep-test that cuts the deck to size: does NOT knowing this cause a support question or a quit? It kills pitch accent, stroke order, onomatopoeia, and counters from the intro, and keeps three-scripts, on-yomi vs kun-yomi, and no-spaces-between-words
- (insight by coach) SYNTHESIS 8 - Trust is an onboarding problem, not a marketing one. Free, no ads, no account, no subscription reads as suspicious; and no account means losing the phone loses everything. A who-made-this card and a backup card belong in the deck BEFORE the user has anything to lose
- (insight by coach) CHOSEN DIRECTIONS - (a) micro-decks fired by trigger rather than one intro dump, (b) a single card registry in user.db powering intro + info affordances + Settings library, (c) cards built from real curriculum items and real review components, (d) three language-nuance cards only: three scripts, on-yomi vs kun-yomi, no spaces
