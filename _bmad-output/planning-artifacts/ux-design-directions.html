Six design direction mockups. Review density: Direction A (compact) is the chosen default. Home: simplified — ring, Review & Learn with icons, streak; SRS stage counts live on Stats. Grammar: hints are progressive (tap to reveal), not shown on the initial prompt.

Direction E
Grammar Review — Progressive Hints
On the initial prompt, cross-domain reinforcement is not shown automatically. A dedicated Hint control reveals more context on each tap (e.g. grammar scope → recent lesson tie-in → full "you learned this yesterday" line). After submission, the result screen can show full explanation and reinforcement without progressive disclosure.
