PakaPaka

Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

You do not need an account to use PakaPaka. Without an account, your progress stays on your device and we receive none of it. An account is optional and is only for syncing your progress between devices.

Without an account

Your lessons, reviews, settings and progress are stored on your device only. We do not receive them.

Our host, Cloudflare, handles each request to this website and to our server. It processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the site and to protect it from abuse. We do not use this data to identify you. We run no analytics or advertising trackers. Cloudflare shows us only totals, such as the number of requests. We cannot see who made them.

With a Google account (optional)

If you choose “Log in with Google”, we store:

  • Your Google account ID. This is a number that Google gives us. It is not your email address or your name.
  • One progress file for your account. It holds your learning progress and settings, so that your devices stay the same.
  • For each signed-in device: a device name (for example “Chrome on macOS”), a hashed sign-in token, and the dates of first and last use.

We use this data only to sign you in and to sync your progress. We do not sell it or share it for advertising.

The web version sets cookies for sign-in. These cookies are strictly necessary: they keep you signed in and show the right buttons. We set no other cookies.

Our legal basis is to provide the sync service you ask for (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).

What we never receive

  • Your email address. We do not store, log or use it.
  • Your AI provider keys (BYOK). They stay in secure storage on your device.
  • Your AI conversations. They stay on your device. Your device sends them directly to the AI provider that you set up, under that provider’s own policy.

Who processes data for us

  • Cloudflare hosts the website and the server, stores your progress file, and checks sign-in requests with Turnstile.
  • Google signs you in. Its own privacy policy applies to your Google account.

Your data may be processed outside your country, including outside the EU. Cloudflare and Google handle such transfers under their own safeguards.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Sign-in tokens expire after 60 days, and we remove expired records on a schedule.

Your rights

  • Delete: When you are signed in, use Delete account in Settings. This removes your account, your progress file and your sign-in records from our server. It also signs out and erases all your devices, and it cannot be undone.
  • Export: Open Settings and use the backup export to download your progress as a file.
  • Access, correct, object or complain: Your data is the same as what the app shows you, and you can change it in the app. For any other request, contact us (below). If you live in the EU or UK, you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

PakaPaka is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we change the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/riyaz942/PakaPaka/issues. Do not put personal data in a public issue. Ask for a private way to reach us instead.
