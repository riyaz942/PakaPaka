# WaniKani kanji mnemonics

Source: WaniKani. Copied as-is for reference.

## Pig

Meaning:
When the moon is bright, it's easy to see that a pig is indeed a pig.

**Hints**
Imagine walking outside at night and seeing a strange oinking animal. You think it's a pig, but just to be sure you grab that thing and hold it up to the moonlight. Yes, definitely a pig! Now you know.

Reading:
Legend has it that if you catch a pig in moonlight, it will tell you its secrets. Sure enough, this pig tells you it's from Bhutan (ぶた).

**Hints**
A pig from Bhutan?! Yes, all pigs are actually from Bhutan, it whispers. Then this Bhutan pig escapes your grip and trots off into the moonlight.

## Rain

Meaning:
The radical for rain and the kanji for rain are the same thing.

**Hints**
Make sure you know your radicals!

Reading:
What if the rain actually tried to aim (あめ) for you? As in, drops would move out of their normal flight path just to hit you on your head? You'd be a lot wetter, that's for sure.

**Hints**
Imagine a world where each rain drop had to choose a target to aim for. How strange would rainfall be? Also, あめ and "aim" don't really sound too much alike, but the goal is to use "aim" to get to あめ, so make sure to take note of that in your mind as you learn the reading.

## Come

Meaning:
The ground is scattered with rice, which will cause the Predator to come because he loves rice. Then once he's gone, the next Predator will come to eat.

**Hints**
This kanji kind of looks like a Predator face, so just imagine Predators come whenever the ground is scattered with rice. They come from all around to grab handfuls of it. Who's next, then? Come forward!

Reading:
When they come, though, they're all disappointed. It turns out it's rye (らい) grains, and rye grains taste terrible. Yuck.

**Hints**
If you love rye grains, on the other hand, imagine them being really happy that they came to get the rice. They're so lucky it's rye!

## Meat

Meaning:
The meat radical and meat are the same in looks as well as in meaning.

**Hints**
Study those radicals, and perhaps we'll eat some meat tonight.

Reading:
You know who loves meat? Nick (にく). You probably know a Nick — imagine him eating some succulent meat right now.

**Hints**
If you don't know anyone named Nick, then just imagine it's Nic Cage. Picture him as he chews that succulent meat, maybe even closing his eyes in gastronomic ecstasy. Yeah. Go for it, Nic. Enjoy that meat.

## Gold

Meaning:
The gold radical and the gold kanji are the same!

**Hints**
Just know your radicals and you'll be golden, so to speak.

Reading:
The most gold in the land belongs to the King (きん) of England. In fact, you've never actually met someone with gold, it all goes straight to the King whenever someone finds some. You just have a few pennies to work with.

**Hints**
Picture the King on a huge pile of gold. Gold coins, gold candelabras, gold ponies. He's laughing and pointing at all of it while making very strong eye contact with you.

## Go

Meaning:
The go radical and the go kanji are exactly the same!

**Hints**
If you know your radicals well enough (and you do), you'll know this kanji.

Reading:
"Oh, did he go again?" When こういち is not around, that's what people often say because he goes to the gyoza (ぎょう) restaurant almost every day. こういち just loves gyoza so much, you always know where he is when he is not home.

**Hints**
Remember, whenever we see the reading こう, think of こういち, a founder of WaniKani (who loves gyoza). We'll keep it consistent, so over time you should develop a character around こういち that you learn to love or hate.

## Behind

Meaning:
If you loiter around to poop in winter you never know what could be happening behind you. After you're done you could turn back to see something really crazy that you missed because you took so long to poop in the cold.

**Hints**
Imagine trying to loiter while you poop in the winter. You're just trying to take your time as you squat over the snow, but soon you hear things behind you.

Reading:
You finally look behind you and see Godzilla (ご) and a giant こういち having a violent battle back there!

**Hints**
Godzilla and こういち were going at it behind you the entire time you were just lazily taking a poop in the snow?! Ahh, that's so embarrassing! And also dangerous, they could have crushed you! You should look behind you more.

## Tea

Meaning:
If you can steal the flowers that adorn a hat worn by an angel, you can use them to make delicious tea. Naturally, angels decorate their hats with only the most aromatic and delicious flowers fit for brewing.

**Hints**
Imagine wrestling with an angel for its flowery hat, then running home with it. Success! Oh yes, this tea is going to taste… *heavenly*. *rimshot*

Reading:
After brewing the tea, you look at the hat again and find a nametag inside that says Chad (ちゃ). You decide that from now on this tea variety will be known as Chad tea, in honor of Chad.

**Hints**
For some reason knowing the angel's name makes you feel extra guilty. Chad the angel, flowerless and hatless... Oh well. At least its name lives on in this heavenly tea.

## Group

Meaning:
Sew thread through your top hat and to the top hats of other people you know and you'll turn into a group of top hat wearing people. You're all connected by the thread now, so everyone will assume you're a group, even if that's all you guys have going for yourselves.

**Hints**
Picture a group of people threaded together by their top hats. Don't you want to be part of a cool group like that? Your own top hat team.

Reading:
Everyone in this group loves cumin (くみ). You know, the spice or seed. The group take off their top hats and fill them with cumin. You're officially a group for lovers of cumin.

**Hints**
What do you do in the cumin group? You smell the cumin. Cook with it. Taste it. This group lives and breathes cumin!

## Paper

Meaning:
The thread that ties you to your clan is paper. Your papers, that is.

**Hints**
The paper itself was also made by your clan using thread woven really tightly together.

Reading:
You need this specific paper to prove that you aren't a Commi (かみ), aka a Communist. Or that you are. It really depends on where you want to go and what decade this is.

**Hints**
Imagine a big guard coming up to you and asking to see your papers so they can see what clan you belong to. Well you're from the Commi clan, so you really hope this guard is okay with that.
