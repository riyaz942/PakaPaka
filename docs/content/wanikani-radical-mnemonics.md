# WaniKani radical mnemonics

Source: WaniKani. Copied as-is for reference.

## Barb

This radical is shaped like a barb. Kind of like the barb you'd see on a barbed fishing hook. Imagine that thing getting stuck in your arm or something. Not exactly pleasant. Say out loud, "Oh dang, I got a barb stuck in me!" Alright, relax, it's only a little barb.

## Big

This radical looks just like a stick figure—more specifically, a really big guy with his arms stretched out nice and big. When you see this radical, just think big, because this guy is really BIG!

## Construction

You know those big steel girders that are used for the frame of big buildings? This is like the end of one of those, and has a very distinct shape, just like this radical. These girders are used in construction, which is why this radical means construction. Funny thing is, you use these girders to put together buildings just like you're going to use this radical to put together kanji!

## Cross

This radical looks like a cross. I mean, just look at it. Two bars going through each other = a cross!

## Drop

This little radical is a drop, maybe of water or some other liquid. Notice how it's only one drop? Sometimes radicals and kanji will have more than one drop in them, but don't let that throw you off.

## Enter

Watch out! This isn't the person radical, it's something else. What's the difference? The right side is longer than the left. This radical also has that extra part sticking out at the top. See it? It kind of looks like a person lowering their head to go inside a tent or something. That's why this radical is enter.

## Fins

These look like fins. What's a pair of disembodied fins doing here?! Some fish must have left them here by accident. Bet they're really missing these fins right about now.

## Ground

This radical consists of a single, horizontal stroke. What's the biggest, single, horizontal stroke? That's the ground. Look at the ground, look at this radical, now look at the ground again. Kind of the same, right?

## Gun

This looks a lot like a gun pointing to the right. You have a handle on the left, and the barrel pointing to the right. Imagine it hanging in a case on the wall in a museum.

## Lid

This looks like something you'd put on a kettle or a pot, right? It even has a little handle to make sure you don't burn yourself. This is some kind of lid.

## Mountain

This is a mountain. See the big peak in the middle and the smaller ones on either side? This totally looks like a mountain.

## Mouth

A big, gaping, square hole represents someone opening their giant mouth to tell you something stupid.

## Nine

If you look at this radical in just the right way, you'll notice that it kind of looks like the letters "n" and "i" (the "i" comes from the little flippy-up stroke on the right side). What do the letters "n" and "i" start spelling? Why, the word nine.

## Person

This is a person. They're standing with their arms down at their sides and their legs spread out, which kind of makes them look like a torso with a pair of legs. Think of 人 as highlighting the bipedal nature of people. Walking on two legs? Probably a person.

## Power

Imagine a guy flexing his arm downward to show off his steroid-pumped arm. It's huge. It's so full of power. RARRRWWWRRR!!

## Prison

This looks like an enclosure of sorts — that's why it's a prison. Imagine a tiny prisoner curled up inside, staring off into space, completely convinced they're trapped forever. Ironically this prison is actually wide open. They're just so consumed with self-pity that they haven't realized they've been free to leave all along.

## River

This radical already looks like a river. See how the lines represent the flow of the river? There's even a slight bend in this river. So when you see this radical, you should think "river."

## Seven

What does this radical look like? Hmm, nothing much. What about if you turn it upside down? Now does it look like a 7 with a line through it? Close enough to help you to remember that this radical is seven, right? Just remember, when you see this radical, flip it over and see what number it looks like. If you can do that, you can remember the meaning!

## Elephant

This is an elephant — or at least, a wannabe elephant. Look closely. It's actually a family of pigs, and the mama is wearing an elephant mask on her head for some reason. See the big ears and trunk on top? She wants to be an elephant so badly that she dressed up like one.

## Lack

A (modified) prison radical containing a person radical. What does a person experience in prison? Lack. Prison is all about lack — lack of freedom, lack of the comforts of home, lack of privacy, lack of conversation. Yep, the life of a person in prison is defined by lack.
