# Kanji bands (plan v2 Phase A)

Generated by `curriculum/_build/reports/kanji_bands.py`. Do not edit by hand.

A band is a teaching budget, not a coverage gate. Kanji outside a learner's band still appear in words — the word is asked for its meaning until every kanji in it reaches guru.
